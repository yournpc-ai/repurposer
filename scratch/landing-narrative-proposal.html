Act 3 · Process

You talk. It works.

One conversation, from raw material to published piece.

You

Bring what you have

Talk video, podcast, slides, or a transcript. Say what you need in the same sentence.

It

Plans and drafts

Posts, clips, articles — written in your style, in the languages you named.

You

Steer in chat

Shorten a post, retitle a clip, push a different angle. Every edit is one message.

It

Ships where you say

LinkedIn, your newsletter, your website — per channel, per language. Nothing ships without your word.

为什么在第三幕：用户不假设工具素养，「怎么工作」必须读成一段关系而不是一份教程——You / It 交替的四步把分工演出来（你只做两件会说话的事：带素材、提意见），回答的是「我要付出什么」，答案越少，转化越近。

Questions, answered

Where is my data stored?

GDPR-ready by design: your uploads and outputs never train third-party models, and you can delete everything at any time.

What can I upload?

Talk videos, podcast audio, slide decks, photos, or just a transcript.

Which languages are supported?

English, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Dutch — written natively, never translated word-for-word.

Does anything get published automatically?

No. Every output waits for your explicit approval first.

Who owns the generated content?

You do, outright. We claim no rights over your outputs.

为什么是商务尾：Pricing / FAQ 不计入叙事幕——它们服务的是已经决定试的人，回答的是采购问题不是叙事问题（与 FLORA 九幕同构：它的定价在付费墙）；GDPR 保持 ready 角度，合规实装前不吹实。
